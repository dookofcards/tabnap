TabNap

Tabs you haven't touched go to sleep. Your memory wakes up.

What it does

TabNap watches how long each tab has been idle and, after the time you choose, asks Chrome to put it to sleep using Chrome's own tab discard. A sleeping tab stays in your tab strip and reloads when you click it.

Why it's different

  • No access to your pages. No "read and change all your data on all websites" warning. TabNap needs only tabs, storage and alarms.
  • No network requests in the free version. No analytics, no ads, no accounts.
  • No remote code. Everything is bundled.
  • Never touches the tab you're looking at, pinned tabs or tabs playing sound (all configurable).
