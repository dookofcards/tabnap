TabNap Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

The short version

TabNap collects no data whatsoever. It has no analytics, no telemetry, no ads and no accounts.

The free version makes no network requests of any kind. If you choose to buy TabNap Pro, payment is handled by ExtensionPay. That is the only situation in which TabNap contacts a server, it happens only after you click "Unlock Pro", and it never sends your settings or anything about your browsing.

What TabNap stores, and where

Your settings (idle time, the sites you protect, Pro timers), a counter of how many tabs TabNap has put to sleep, and a local flag recording whether Pro is unlocked are stored in your browser's extension storage on your device. If you are signed into Chrome with extension sync enabled, Chrome may sync your settings between your own devices through your Google account. That sync is performed by Chrome, not by TabNap, and the data never reaches us. We operate no servers.

What TabNap accesses, and why

  • Tabs: TabNap reads each open tab's address, when it was last used, and whether it is pinned, playing sound or in a group. It does this on your device only, to decide which tabs have been idle and which sites you protected, and then asks Chrome to put those tabs to sleep. Tab addresses are never stored, logged or transmitted. TabNap cannot read the content of any page and has no host access and no content scripts on websites.
  • Storage: to keep the settings described above.
  • Alarms: a once-a-minute timer that wakes TabNap to check for idle tabs.

Payment (only if you buy Pro)

Purchases are processed by ExtensionPay and Stripe. If you buy Pro, they receive your email address and payment details under their privacy policies. TabNap never sees your card details. To confirm a purchase, TabNap includes a small script that runs only on extensionpay.com. If you never buy Pro, no request is ever made and nothing is ever sent.

Data sharing

None. TabNap shares nothing with anyone. The only third party involved at all is the payment processor above, and only if you choose to pay.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this URL with an updated effective date.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, or privacy concerns? Open an issue at github.com/dookofcards/tabnap/issues. Issues are public, so please don't include anything sensitive.
