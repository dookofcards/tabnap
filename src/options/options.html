TabNap

Sleeps tabs you haven't touched, using Chrome's own tab discard. It never reads your pages. The free version makes no network requests.

When to sleep tabs

A sleeping tab stays in the tab strip and reloads when you click it. The tab you are looking at is never put to sleep.

Protected sites

These sites never sleep (subdomains included).

Your protected sites and per-site timers are on hold: TabNap can't see tab addresses, so it leaves tabs awake rather than risk sleeping a protected one.
